# Operating model

## North Star per company

| Company | North Star (12–36 months) |
|---|---|
| Custom95 | €3M revenue, 12–15% margin, recurring via brandshops/portals/key accounts, runs without the founder, exit-ready |
| Brand95 | Repeatable brand-building engine; multiple €500k+ ARR brands with brand managers |
| Soul Startup Studio | 1–2 launched startups with real traction; fewer bets, higher conviction |
| Student95 | Own the student-association merch market as a separate brand |
| PortaPay | Validate before building; lean |

## Rhythm

| When | What | Command |
|---|---|---|
| Daily (10 min) | Decide what's waiting, get one next step per unit in focus | `/review`, `/next`, then `/execute` |
| Weekly (Mon, 45 min) | CEO review: bottlenecks, KPIs, experiments, pipeline, 3 priorities per company | `/weekly` |
| Weekly (sales) | Research batch → drafts → batch approval → send → follow-ups | `/research-leads`, `/outreach`, `/send-approved` |
| Monthly | Finance close, ops review, initiative reset | `/finance-review`, `/ops-review` |
| Quarterly | Portfolio: park/kill/fund decisions on ventures and brands | `/weekly` + `jarvis unit park|kill` |

## Focus rules (the engine enforces or warns)

1. Max **3 active initiatives** per unit.
2. Brand95: **one brand in Build** (brand → launch stages), **one in Discover/Validate**. Others wait at Intake.
3. **Max two ventures past Intake** across the studio.
4. Two failed experiments in one stage → **park**, don't rationalize a third.
5. Anything the founder does twice → SOP candidate; anything the founder does weekly → owner candidate.
6. If more than 5 units need founder attention in a week, that is over-parallelization: delegate or park.

## What "running" means for a department

A department is running when: KPIs are inside the target band, health checks pass, cadence items happened, founder hours are at target. The `dept-*` blueprints define these; `jarvis unit <ref>` shows them; the engine flags misses.

## What "validated" means

Gate criteria are behavioral: signups, reservations, commitments, paid pilots, stockists, reorders. Compliments are not evidence. Criteria get marked with a link or one line of proof, and stages advance only when the gate is complete.
